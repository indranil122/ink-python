## The task

Write `describe_temperature(celsius)` returning a description.

| Celsius | Result |
| --- | --- |
| at or below 0 | `"freezing"` |
| 1 to 10 | `"cold"` |
| 11 to 20 | `"mild"` |
| 21 to 30 | `"warm"` |
| above 30 | `"hot"` |

## Rules

- Boundaries belong to the colder band: 10 is `"cold"`, 20 is `"mild"`.
- Floats and integers both work.

## The lesson

This is a chain of five conditions, which is the point: real code is mostly
long chains. Write it top to bottom from the strictest threshold, and resist the
urge to use a dictionary lookup, which cannot express `<=` without extra work.
